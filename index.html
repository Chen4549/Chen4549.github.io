About Me

Hi, I'm from Taiwan and have studied in the US for almost a decade.

I've been doing research in the medical AI field, and the following are my focuses:

  • Multimodal learning
  • Agentic systems
  • Model interpretability & safety

The following are the professors I've worked with or am currently working with:

  • Dr. Tianyu Han, University of Pennsylvania
  • Dr. Yiqiu (Artie) Shen, New York University

Research Interests

Multimodal learning

Utilizing multiple modalities, such as MRI, mammography, ultrasound, X-ray and clinical records, to improve models.

Agentic systems

Clinical agents that reason across radiology reports, patient history and medical imaging to support diagnosis and prognosis.

Interpretability & safety

Interpreting the reasoning behind model decisions and exposing vulnerabilities in medical agent systems.
